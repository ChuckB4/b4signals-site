Market Scanning. Multi-Instrument Awareness. B4 Trading Intelligence.

B4 Scanner is a NinjaTrader 8 add-on designed to help traders monitor multiple instruments and identify market conditions that warrant further review within the B4 Signals trading methodology.

Instead of manually reviewing every instrument and chart, B4 Scanner provides a dedicated scanning interface for surfacing relevant instruments, developing conditions, and potential setups across the supported market universe. It helps traders focus their attention where additional analysis may be useful while keeping final trading decisions grounded in confirmation and risk management.

Core Capabilities

  • Multi-Instrument Scanning: Monitors supported instruments for market conditions relevant to the configured B4 scanning workflow.
  • B4 Condition Monitoring: Helps surface instruments exhibiting relevant analytical conditions based on available data and implemented scanning criteria.
  • Setup Discovery: Brings potential trading scenarios to the trader's attention for further investigation rather than treating scan results as automatic trade instructions.
  • Market Context: Supports a more organized review of instruments and developing conditions within the broader B4 Signals methodology.
  • B4 Ecosystem Compatibility: Designed as a complementary NinjaTrader 8 add-on alongside B4 Algo Pro, B4 Tape, and B4 L2 DOM Pro, working together as one B4 workflow.
  • Dedicated Scanning Workflow: Provides a focused interface for reviewing scan results without requiring the trader to inspect every supported instrument individually.

Find What Deserves Your Attention

Markets do not develop opportunities at the same time. B4 Scanner helps traders organize their market review by highlighting instruments and conditions that may deserve closer examination.

Scanner results are informational. A surfaced instrument or condition is not, by itself, a confirmed trade, an entry instruction, or a guarantee of a profitable outcome. Traders should evaluate the underlying chart, applicable B4 confirmation requirements, market context, and risk controls before acting.
