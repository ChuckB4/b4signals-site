Advanced Ichimoku Trend Structure and Market Analysis

B4 Shoyu is a specialized B4 Signals trading tool focused on Ichimoku-based trend structure, market direction, and price-action context. It is designed to help traders evaluate the relationship between price, trend structure, and potential areas of support and resistance within the broader B4 analytical framework.

By focusing on Ichimoku principles and their application to market structure, B4 Shoyu provides a dedicated approach to interpreting trend conditions, assessing directional bias, and evaluating how price behaves relative to key structural levels.

Core Capabilities

  • Ichimoku Trend Structure: Analyzes Ichimoku-based components to help evaluate trend direction, price positioning, and evolving market structure.
  • Directional Market Context: Helps traders assess whether market conditions support a bullish, bearish, or less clearly defined directional bias.
  • Price and Structural Relationships: Supports the interpretation of price relative to Ichimoku components and relevant trend boundaries.
  • Support and Resistance Context: Helps identify structural areas that may influence price behavior and potential trading decisions.
  • B4 Analytical Integration: Designed to complement the broader B4 Signals methodology, where compatible, alongside B4 Algo, B4 Tape, and other B4 trading tools.
  • Platform Availability: Available for TradingView and NinjaTrader 8, with features that reflect the capabilities of each platform.

Built for Structured Market Interpretation

B4 Shoyu focuses on understanding market structure rather than treating individual indicator readings as isolated trade signals. Its role is to help traders interpret trend conditions and evaluate price behavior within an Ichimoku-based framework.
