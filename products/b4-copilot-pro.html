Bring any compatible indicator

B4 CoPilot Pro is not limited to B4 indicators. It is an open signal-integration and execution platform: any indicator that speaks the B4 Signal API can drive it. Add the indicator to your chart, and CoPilot Pro finds it, reads its signals and puts them to work under your rules.

The API publishes signals, not orders. Your indicator says what it sees. CoPilot Pro checks your rules and handles the execution.

  • B4BuySignals and B4SellSignals: the indicator's long and short entry signals, bar by bar.
  • B4ConfirmationDirections: its directional read, used to confirm or hold back a trade.
  • B4UseEntrySignals and B4UseConfirmations: two switches that tell CoPilot Pro to use the indicator for entries, for confirmation, or for both.

Five members, one contract. B4 tools, third-party indicators and your own code all plug in the same way.

Free resources for developers

Build your own signal sources with the free B4 Indicators Developer Kit, the B4 Developer Repository and B4 Studio, the B4 indicator and strategy builder that generates preview templates you can take further. Every indicator you build can communicate with CoPilot Pro.

Order-flow intelligence, built in

CoPilot Pro reads the market the way a tape reader does, with order-flow intelligence from B4 Order Flow and B4 Auction Pro.

  • The tape: 36 tape-reading detectors, including sweeps, absorption, refills, stop runs, delta flips, tape speed and exhaustion, with size tiers and spoken callouts.
  • The DOM and Level II: stacking and pulling, liquidity walls and order-book imbalance.
  • Footprint and auction: bid and ask volume, delta, imbalances and stacked imbalances, volume profile with POC and value area, TPO, CVD and CVD divergence, and liquidity zones.
  • Market commentary: a 224-statement spoken commentary library that tells you what the market is doing, in plain English.

Before an entry goes through, CoPilot Pro's tape filters check aggression, absorption, stops and refills, sweeps, room to the next level, and tape bias and freshness.

Confirmation on your terms

Decide what has to line up before a trade is taken. CoPilot Pro waits until it does.

  • Entry signals from CoPilot Pro's built-in order-flow setups and from every connected indicator.
  • Independent confirmations: require chart indicators, delta momentum, Level II delta and force, stacked imbalances, pivots, session delta or rising volume to agree.
  • Order-flow confirmation from the tape filters.
  • Trend and market-condition filters: trade trending markets, ranging markets, or let CoPilot Pro choose automatically.
  • Number of signals to confirm: one or more, X or more, or all.

Build sophisticated strategies without writing an execution system.

Machine-learning signals from B4 Algo Pro

B4 Algo Pro brings machine-learning signals into the workflow. They reach CoPilot Pro through the B4 Signal API, where they are confirmed and executed under your rules, like any other signal source.

Execution and trade management

  • Automated strategy execution: run a single trade, once a day or continuously, or pause with one click.
  • Long, short or both: switch each side on or off.
  • NinjaTrader ATM strategy integration: stops and targets are placed and managed by the ATM strategy you choose.
  • Opposite-signal handling: exit, reverse, move to break-even, or stay in the trade.
  • Position management: break-even plus ticks, and daily loss and profit limits that stand the strategy down.
  • Entry controls and discipline: trading windows and trading days, one entry per account and instrument, entries only when flat, and an entry log with the reason for every signal taken or not taken.
