Introducing a New Era of AI-Powered Trading Intelligence.

Coming This Black Friday

B4 Quantum is the next evolution of the B4 Signals ecosystem: an AI trade desk built to read the market the way a disciplined professional does—gathering the evidence, weighing it, explaining it, and acting only when the case is strong enough.

It brings the entire B4 family into one intelligent workspace. B4 Algo Pro, B4 Shoyu, the B4 PY Indicator, B4 Squeeze, B4 Wave, footprint, volume profile, and B4 Tape's order-flow evidence feed a single decision engine, while B4 CoPilot Pro—the B4 strategy execution engine—turns confirmed decisions into rules-based action.

Fast enough to watch everything. Disciplined enough to wait.

One Desk. Every Signal. One Decision.

  • AI Market Briefing: Start each session with an AI-built map of the day: prior highs and lows, the overnight range, value areas, VWAP, poor highs and lows, and unfinished auctions worth watching.
  • Evidence Fusion Engine: Combines B4 indicator signals, Ichimoku structure, P and Y patterns, squeeze conditions, wave structure, footprint, volume profile, and tape events into one scored view of the market.
  • Explainable Decisions: Every call arrives with its reasoning—what the evidence shows, what confirmation is still missing, and what would invalidate the idea. No black boxes.
  • Market-Regime Detection: Recognizes trending, rotational, and volatile conditions, and adapts which setups and filters it trusts.
  • Event Memory: Follows developing scenarios over time—an unfinished auction, a failed breakout, a shift in liquidity—and reports when each one confirms, fails, or expires.
  • News and Sentiment Awareness: Adds headline and sentiment context to the technical picture, so a clean chart setup is not taken blind into a news shock.
  • Data-Quality Guardrails: B4 Quantum knows when not to trade. Missing, degraded, or estimated data blocks a decision instead of being guessed around.

From Insight to Automated Execution

  • Automation Through B4 CoPilot Pro: Confirmed decisions route to the B4 strategy execution engine, which applies your configured signals, filters, and confirmation rules.
  • Built-In Risk Management: Stops and targets through NinjaTrader ATM strategies, break-even and trailing logic, position sizing, and session risk limits—defined before any trade is taken.
  • Signal, Setup, Trade—Kept Distinct: A signal firing, a setup developing, confirmation arriving, and an order executing are tracked as separate events, so you always know exactly why a trade happened.
  • Practice Mode: Run any automated workflow on NinjaTrader's simulation and playback accounts to see exactly how the desk trades before you go further.

Integrated with B4 Tape and B4 L2 DOM Pro

  • B4 Tape — The Desk's Eyes and Voice: B4 Quantum reads B4 Tape's order-flow evidence—big trades, absorption, tape speed, liquidity walls and vacuums, and its 130+ absorption and market-microstructure setups. Desk decisions appear in B4 Tape's Trade Engine panel and are designed to be explained through the audio squawk box. B4 Tape stays informational; it never places orders.
  • B4 L2 DOM Pro — Liquidity and Control: Level II depth, stacking and pulling, liquidity migration, and order-book imbalance give the desk a live view of where liquidity sits and how it is moving. When you trade by hand, B4 L2 DOM Pro is where you act on the desk's decision, with stops, targets, break-even, trailing, and flatten controls in one place.
  • One Shared Decision Record: B4 Tape, B4 L2 DOM Pro, B4 Scanner, and B4 CoPilot Pro are designed to read the same decision record—so every tool shows the same reasoning, the same status, and the same "trade blocked" when the data is not good enough.
  • Clear Roles, No Overlap: Quantum decides, B4 Tape explains, B4 L2 DOM Pro gives you hands-on control, and B4 CoPilot Pro executes. Each component keeps its own job, so automation never hides inside an informational tool.

The Quantum Research Lab

  • Strategy Research and Validation: Evaluate strategies on the same evidence the live desk uses, with honest, out-of-sample testing instead of curve-fitting.
  • Pre-Registered Forward Testing: Lock a strategy's rules before the test begins, then measure it on new sessions with no tuning along the way.
  • Machine-Learning Models: Train and score B4 models on recorded market evidence, with every model's training status shown—never assumed.
  • Outcome Tracking: Every observation and decision is recorded with its result, building an evidence base that sharpens the desk session after session.
  • Replay and Review: Study past sessions with the desk's reasoning replayed alongside price, so every trade—taken or skipped—becomes a lesson.

Built on the B4 Philosophy

Powerful analytical tools. Structured process. Meaningful evidence. Disciplined execution. B4 Quantum is designed to bring all four together in one AI-driven trade desk—and to keep getting smarter as the B4 ecosystem grows.

Two Ways to Run the Desk

  • The Intelligence Desk: AI-powered analysis with you making the call. Every decision explained, every level mapped, every setup scored.
  • The Automated Desk: Confirmed decisions executed through B4 CoPilot Pro under your rules, with the Quantum Research Lab working behind the scenes.
