Introducing a New Era of AI-Powered Trading Intelligence.

Coming soon

B4 Quantum is the next evolution of the B4 Signals ecosystem: an AI trade desk built to read the market the way a disciplined professional does—gathering the evidence, weighing it, explaining it, and acting only when the case is strong enough.

It brings the entire B4 family into one intelligent workspace. B4 Algo Pro, B4 Indicator Pro, B4 Ichimoku, B4 Auction Pro and the order-flow evidence from B4 Order Flow feed a single decision engine, while B4 CoPilot Pro—the B4 strategy execution engine—turns confirmed decisions into rules-based action.

Fast enough to watch everything. Disciplined enough to wait.

One Desk. Every Signal. One Decision.

  • AI Market Briefing: Live news and sentiment scoring for every instrument you follow, from a financial-language AI model (FinBERT), shown and spoken in the news strip of B4 Tape, right beside the B4 levels on your chart: VWAP, value areas and the auctions worth watching.
  • Evidence Fusion Engine: Every B4 indicator publishes to one shared signal bus, the B4 Context Bus. B4 Scanner brings it together for each instrument (scenario, fired state, trend, VWAP, footprint, delta, CVD, Ichimoku and squeeze) into one confluence view of the market.
  • Explainable Decisions: Every call arrives with its reasoning. A Scanner row tells you why a setup is active, what is still missing and what would cancel it; B4 Tape explains every call it speaks in plain English; and the research lab writes a report with the reason each strategy idea passed or failed every gate. No black boxes.
  • Market-Regime Detection: Recognizes trending, rotational, and volatile conditions, and adapts which setups and filters it trusts.
  • Event Memory: Follows developing scenarios over time—an unfinished auction, a failed breakout, a shift in liquidity—and reports when each one confirms, fails, or expires.
  • News and Sentiment Awareness: The same news and sentiment scores sit next to the technical picture, so a clean chart setup is not taken blind into a news shock.
  • Data-Quality Guardrails: B4 Quantum knows when not to trade. Missing, degraded, or estimated data blocks a decision instead of being guessed around.

19 AI Bots. One Research Team.

  • An AI Research Team: 19 AI bots running on local AI models propose strategy ideas, critique each other's work and build the strongest ideas into exact, testable trading rules.
  • Five Strict Validation Gates: Every idea must pass five gates, in order: (1) training data, (2) unseen validation data, (3) stable settings, (4) robustness and walk-forward testing, and (5) a final check on market data kept sealed from the AI. Fail one gate and the idea never reaches you.
  • A Written Reason for Every Verdict: The research lab reports why each idea passed or failed each gate, so nothing is accepted on faith.
  • Signed, Versioned Releases: Only strategies that clear every gate are signed and released as versioned updates. Nothing unsigned ever runs, and an update never rolls you back to an older version.

From Insight to Automated Execution

  • Automated Trading: B4 Quantum runs each signed strategy for you: entries, exits, stop and target, exactly by its rules.
  • Automation Through B4 CoPilot Pro: Confirmed decisions route to the B4 strategy execution engine, which applies your configured signals, filters, and confirmation rules.
  • Built-In Risk Management: A daily loss limit, a trailing drawdown stop and a cool-down after a losing streak, plus stops and targets through NinjaTrader ATM strategies, break-even and trailing logic, all defined before any trade is taken.
  • Signal, Setup, Trade—Kept Distinct: A signal firing, a setup developing, confirmation arriving, and an order executing are tracked as separate events, so you always know exactly why a trade happened.

Integrated with B4 Order Flow

  • The Tape — The Desk's Eyes and Voice: B4 Quantum reads the order-flow evidence from the tape in B4 Order Flow—big trades, absorption, tape speed, liquidity walls and vacuums. Desk decisions are designed to appear in the tape's decision panel and to be explained through the audio squawk box. The tape stays informational; it never places orders.
  • The DOM — Liquidity and Control: Level II depth, stacking and pulling, liquidity migration, and order-book imbalance give the desk a live view of where liquidity sits and how it is moving. When you trade by hand, the DOM in B4 Order Flow is where you act on the desk's decision, with stop, break-even, trailing, exit and flatten controls in one place.
  • One Shared Decision Record: B4 Order Flow and B4 CoPilot Pro are designed to read the same decision record—so every tool shows the same reasoning, the same status, and the same "trade blocked" when the data is not good enough.
  • Clear Roles, No Overlap: Quantum decides, B4 Order Flow explains and gives you hands-on control, and B4 CoPilot Pro executes. Each component keeps its own job, so automation never hides inside an informational tool.

The Quantum Research Lab

  • Strategy Research and Validation: Evaluate strategies on the same evidence the live desk uses, with honest, out-of-sample testing instead of curve-fitting.
  • Pre-Registered Forward Testing: Lock a strategy's rules before the test begins, then measure it on new sessions with no tuning along the way.
  • Machine-Learning Models: Train and score B4 models on recorded market evidence, with every model's training status shown—never assumed.
  • Outcome Tracking: Every observation and decision is recorded with its result, building an evidence base that sharpens the desk session after session.
  • Replay and Review: Study past sessions with the desk's reasoning replayed alongside price, so every trade—taken or skipped—becomes a lesson.

Built on the B4 Philosophy

Powerful analytical tools. Structured process. Meaningful evidence. Disciplined execution. B4 Quantum is designed to bring all four together in one AI-driven trade desk—with new signed strategies added as the research lab clears them.

Two Ways to Run the Desk

  • The Intelligence Desk: AI-powered analysis with you making the call. Every decision explained, every level mapped, every setup scored.
  • The Automated Desk: Confirmed decisions executed through B4 CoPilot Pro under your rules, with the Quantum Research Lab working behind the scenes.
