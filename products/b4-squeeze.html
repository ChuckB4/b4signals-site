B4 Auction Pro

See the auction: where the volume traded and who was in control at every price.

  • Footprint chart: bid and ask volume at every price, with delta and imbalances
  • Volume profile with point of control and value area, plus a per-bar B4 value area built on the classic CBOT auction method
  • B4 Order Flow Markers from the tape drawn on the profile chart, and TPO time-at-price with one on/off switch
  • Four ready color palettes, a squeeze module, a CVD divergence readout, optional CVD confirmation and signal outputs for B4 CoPilot Pro

B4 Footprint Pro is now B4 Auction Pro. Members use B4 Footprint Pro (the current build) today. B4 Auction Pro, with the volume profile, TPO, squeeze, CVD divergence and CVD confirmation modules and the new liquidity tools, arrives with the B4 V4 release.

What's inside (43)

  • B4 Auction Pro footprint · formerly B4 Auction Pro See the bid and ask volume at every price inside each bar.
  • Session and custom volume profiles Previous, current and custom-range profiles show where volume built up.
  • Per-bar B4 value area Shades each bar's value area so you see where most of its volume traded.
  • Imbalances, stacked imbalances Highlights prices where one side dominated and marks stacked runs as zones.
  • TPO time-at-price Market profile letters show where price spent time, with initial balance and single prints.
  • TPO signals (IB, poor highs / lows, single prints) Flags IB breakouts, poor highs and lows, single prints and value-area tests.
  • CVD confirmation · formerly B4 CVD Optional filter that keeps a profile setup only when a matching CVD divergence appears.
  • CUM delta divergence readout Shows on the dashboard when cumulative delta and price disagree.
  • Prop Desk dashboard VWAP bands, opening range, bid / ask pressure, POC migration and large lots in one panel.
  • B4 Squeeze zone · formerly B4 Squeeze (3-minute squeeze zone) The B4 Squeeze built from this chart's own trades, with no extra data series.
  • Depth heatmap (B4 Book) Paints resting limit orders over time so you see where size sits. Needs Level 2.
  • Flow heatmap (depth + prints) Resting depth, big-print bubbles and a delta strip in one view. Needs Level 2.
  • Liquidity (Level 2) Marks meaningful resting levels and shows whether they hold, weaken or break.
  • Liquidity zones Zones where heavy aggressive volume traded but price could not get through.
  • Smart Exits Draws a structure-based stop and up to three targets from your chart's levels, then trails it.
  • Virgin POC (B4) Marks bar POCs that price has not traded back through yet.
  • MTF vPOC (B4) Virgin POCs from 5-minute to 4-hour timeframes, with lined-up levels highlighted.
  • HVN High-volume nodes drawn as bands inside each profile.
  • Up to 8 anchored VWAPs Anchor each VWAP to the session, week, month, a swing or a time you pick.
  • Auto Levels Prior day, overnight, RTH open, pivots and prior week and month levels, drawn for you.
  • Opening Range (ORB) The opening range high and low, timed for your instrument.
  • Opening Drive Marks a strong one-way move in the first minutes of the day session.
  • Trapped Traders Flags bars where aggressive buyers or sellers got caught at a new extreme.
  • Aggression Arrows Arrows when trading turns unusually intense for the time of day and one side dominates.
  • Exhaustion & Absorption Spots pushes that run out of delta, and big delta that fails to move price.
  • Leg-to-Leg Profile A volume or delta profile of the current swing leg, with its HVN and LVN.
  • Momentum Deceleration Warns when a new extreme is made on delta that shrinks bar after bar.
  • Large Prints & Sweeps Bubbles for big single trades and marks for fast sweeps through several prices.
  • Confluence Fires when two or more of your chosen signals agree within a few bars.
  • Range Projection Projects today's high and low targets from the average range of recent sessions.
  • Pullback Zones Shades the 50 to 61.8 percent retracement band after a rotation.
  • Setups, risk and trade tracking Each profile setup gets an entry, stop and target, then is tracked to the outcome.
  • Confidence Engine Scores every setup from about 0 to 10 using delta, imbalances, VWAP and more.
  • Footer Table A stats table under the bars: delta, session delta, volume and any rows you pick.
  • Tape Strip A running strip of the latest large trades right on the chart.
  • Macro Sessions Colors candles by the shape of the current session's profile, long or short.
  • Candle Coloring Colors price bars by the direction of the active profile setup.
  • Four color palettes The B4 palette or classic colors, with a light-chart version that keeps text readable.
  • B4 Order Flow Markers The levels and setups B4 Order Flow Tape calls out, drawn as lines on your chart.
  • B4 Bars footprint Keeps the footprint accurate on B4 Bars, shading untraded opens and flagging data mismatches.
  • Swing Labels Labels each swing leg with its size, volume and optional delta.
  • Alerts NinjaTrader alerts when key profile shapes form or price touches a setup entry.
