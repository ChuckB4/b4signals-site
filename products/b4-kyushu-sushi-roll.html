Kyushu Legs. Trend Cloud. Consolidation Zones.

B4 Kyushu Sushi Roll is a TradingView add-on to the B4 Ichimoku built on Kyushu Ashi, the simplified Ichimoku method introduced by Goichi Hosoda. Kyushu Ashi uses Ichimoku time cycles to find trend continuations and reversals, and it is meant to be read together with the five Ichimoku lines, not on its own.

B4 Kyushu Sushi Roll shows the Kyushu signals in a simple form: up and down carets on the legs, a trend cloud for the prevailing trend, and colored candles where no Kyushu signal is present, so consolidation, continuation and reversal areas are easy to see.

Core Capabilities

  • Kyushu Legs: draws the Kyushu legs on the chart.
  • Leg Signals: marks the Kyushu leg signals with up and down carets.
  • Trend Cloud: a cloud for the prevailing trend, based on the Kyushu legs.
  • No-Signal Candles: colors candles with no Kyushu signal to show consolidation.
  • Consolidation Alerts: alerts when no Kyushu signal is present.
  • Consolidation Zones: draws dynamic consolidation zones for breakout identification.

Reading It With the Ichimoku

Kyushu Ashi is not meant to be traded on its own. Read the Kyushu legs and the trend cloud together with the five Ichimoku lines and price action before acting on a signal.
