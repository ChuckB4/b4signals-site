Advanced Order Flow. Bid/Ask Analysis. Market Participation.

B4 Footprint Pro is an advanced order-flow analysis tool designed to help traders examine buying and selling activity at individual price levels. By presenting detailed footprint information, it provides a granular view of executed trading activity to help evaluate order-flow imbalances, potential absorption, aggressive participation, and changes in market behavior.

Built for traders who want to look beyond conventional candles and volume bars, B4 Footprint Pro focuses on the interaction between buyers and sellers within each bar, helping reveal details that may otherwise be difficult to interpret from price action alone.

Core Capabilities

  • Bid/Ask Footprint Analysis: Examines executed volume at individual price levels, separating buying and selling activity where supported by the underlying data.
  • Delta Analysis: Helps evaluate the difference between buying and selling volume to assess directional order-flow pressure.
  • Imbalance Detection: Identifies qualifying differences between bid and ask activity where the configured calculations support imbalance analysis.
  • Absorption Analysis: Supports the investigation of situations where substantial trading activity occurs without corresponding price progress, subject to the implemented detection methodology.
  • Aggressive Buying and Selling: Helps traders evaluate the intensity and distribution of executed buying and selling activity across price levels.
  • Price Rejection and Acceptance: Adds order-flow context for evaluating how the market responds at important prices, including potential rejection, continuation, and changes in directional pressure.
  • Market Structure Context: Complements price action, volume distribution, and other technical analysis when evaluating potential entries, exits, and developing trade conditions.
  • B4 Ecosystem Compatibility: Designed to complement compatible B4 Signals tools, including B4 Algo Pro, B4 Tape, and B4 Volume Profile.

Examine the Activity Behind Price

Price movement tells only part of the story. Footprint analysis provides a closer look at the executed trading activity occurring within that movement.

B4 Footprint Pro helps traders investigate whether buying or selling pressure is concentrated at particular prices, whether activity is producing meaningful price progress, and whether developing order flow supports further analysis of a potential setup.

Footprint readings must be interpreted in context. Delta, imbalance, and apparent absorption do not independently establish future direction or guarantee a successful trade.
