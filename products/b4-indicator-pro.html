Precision Squeeze Detection. Explosive Momentum. Breakout Intelligence.

Find the compression. Read the momentum. Attack the breakout.

B4 Indicator Pro is an advanced momentum and breakout indicator for NinjaTrader 8. It helps you identify compression, detect developing momentum and expose potential volatility expansion, and it gives you more than one way to read a developing directional move.

It is built on MACD-based Bollinger Bands, squeeze and release detection, Trend Line analysis, a momentum histogram and overbought and oversold zones, all in one lower chart panel. Together they give you a framework for before, during and after a momentum breakout.

Advanced Squeeze Detection

B4 Indicator Pro uses B4's own adaptive squeeze. A squeeze starts when the MACD-BB band width ranks among the narrowest 15 percent of its last 500 bars, and it ends only when the width is back above the 35 percent mark, so it holds steady instead of flickering on and off. All three values are settings. The panel follows each squeeze from the first compressed bar to the release.

  • Developing compression: a squeeze dot on the zero line on every squeeze bar, the bands change color, and an optional squeeze cloud fills the space between them.
  • Sustained squeeze: the run of dots shows how long the compression has lasted, and the dots are colored and sized normal, strong or extreme as the bands tighten.
  • Release points: a larger release marker with a ring on the bar the squeeze fires, colored bullish, bearish or uncertain.
  • Directional momentum out of consolidation: the release direction comes from the Trend Line's slope and the momentum histogram together.
  • Confirmed or not: optional text markers on the price bars (off by default) show whether a release was confirmed (a close beyond the squeeze range, with the bands widening and momentum agreeing), unconfirmed, failed or reversed.
  • Short squeezes filtered: a squeeze shorter than the minimum length (2 bars by default) is treated as a flicker and gets no release.
  • Weakening momentum: the Trend Line and the histogram switch to their weakening colors when a move starts to lose strength.
  • Continuation or reversal: read what follows the release in the Trend Line, the bands and the histogram.

A squeeze is not a trade by itself.

Breakout Intelligence

When a squeeze releases, B4 Indicator Pro shows what the breakout is doing: its direction, its strength and how the Trend Line behaves around the bands.

  • Release markers colored by direction
  • Markers where the Trend Line crosses the top band, the midline or the bottom band
  • Markers where the Trend Cloud starts or is lost, as momentum transitions
  • Optional alerts for those band crosses and for MACD-BB crossing a level you choose: zero, lower, middle or upper

Use it for entry timing, confirmation and continuation, alongside your own plan.

The Gray Trendline Setup

The Trend Line has five states: bullish strengthening, bullish weakening, gray, bearish weakening and bearish strengthening. When the Trend Line turns gray, momentum is neutral or transitional: it is inside the bands, or flat at a band. When it moves back across a relevant band, a new opportunity may develop. Monitor:

  • the transition into gray
  • where the Trend Line sits relative to the top band, the midline and the bottom band
  • the return across a band, marked on the panel
  • the direction of the momentum histogram
  • agreement with the broader trend from the Fib SuperTrend and the RSI Inverse Fisher Transform
  • support from a squeeze release

The complete setup, not a color change in isolation.

Multiple Entry Opportunities

Three ways to read the same panel. They are reading methods, not separate signals or settings:

  • Aggressive: early participation, as the Trend Line curls, a squeeze releases or the Trend Line moves back across a band.
  • Confirmation: the Trend Line, the bands and the histogram agree. The B4 Buy and Sell triangles on the price chart appear only when the trend, the Trend Line beyond its band and the Trend Cloud all agree.
  • Conservative: an established direction: the vertical event line has marked the trend turn, the Trend Cloud is in place and the Trend Line is strengthening.

Momentum Histogram

The MACD (12, 26, 9) histogram in four colors: positive and rising, positive, negative and falling, and negative. It shows:

  • acceleration and deceleration of momentum
  • positive and negative momentum
  • how momentum changes as a breakout develops
  • agreement or conflict with the Trend Line
  • a move losing strength

Overbought and Oversold Intelligence

Six zone lines frame the extremes of the Trend Line: Overbought, Trail Top and Turbulence Zone Top above zero, and Turbulence Zone Bottom, Trail Bottom and Oversold below, with an optional fill and labels. Built-in values for ES, MES, NQ and MNQ, and you can set your own levels on any chart.

An extreme is not an automatic reversal signal. Strong trends can stay extended.

B4 Surge Analysis

An alternate view of the same momentum. A B4 Surge is a run of bars in which the Trend Line pushes one way. Each Surge bar's height shows how much volume drove the push, measured against what is normal for that time of day, so a surge at the open and a surge at lunch are on the same scale. A Surge bar turns half-transparent when the push is fading. The Calm Zone shades the bars where the Trend Line is neutral: inside the bands or flat. Show B4 Surge on its own or inside the standard panel.

  • Calm tolerance: High ends a Surge at the first Calm Zone bar, Low tolerates one Calm Zone bar inside a run
  • Compare volume with the same time of day, in 1, 5, 15 or 30-minute slots, or turn the comparison off
  • A B4 Surge color scheme for the whole panel

Momentum Failure

When a move starts to fail, the panel shows it: the Trend Line turns from strengthening to weakening, the histogram fades, the Trend Cloud is lost, or the Trend Line curls against the move.

Treat it as a warning condition, not a certainty.

Expanded Alerts

Configurable NinjaTrader alerts, off by default, each with its own switch, a master switch and an optional sound:

  • Squeeze conditions: Squeeze Start and Scalper Squeeze, plus a release alert for confirmed, failed and reversed releases
  • Entries: Long Entry and Short Entry on the Buy and Sell triangles
  • Band and level crossings: MACD-BB crossed up or down at zero, lower, middle or upper, and the Trend Line crossing the top band, the midline or the bottom band
  • Momentum changes: curl pivot, Trend Cloud started and Trend Cloud lost
