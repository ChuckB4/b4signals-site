More Than a Tape Reader. A Tape-Reading Coach.

B4 Tape combines market information, price levels, indicator signals, and order-flow evidence with an integrated audio squawk box designed to help traders understand what the market is doing and why it matters.

The audio experience goes beyond announcing that something has happened. Through configurable spoken commentary, B4 Tape can help explain relevant market conditions, identify important price levels, communicate developing order-flow evidence, and reinforce the principles of professional tape reading.

Whether you are learning to interpret buying and selling pressure or refining your ability to recognize important market behavior, B4 Tape is designed to help turn market information into understandable, actionable context for your own analysis.

The Audio Squawk Box: Your Real-Time Tape-Reading Coach

  • Spoken Market Commentary: Hear relevant market events and analytical conditions communicated through audio.
  • Tape-Reading Education: Learn to interpret market activity, price behavior, and order-flow evidence through contextual explanations.
  • Price-Level Awareness: Receive commentary about relevant levels and developing conditions, where supported by the configured data and signals.
  • Order-Flow Interpretation: Better understand buying and selling pressure, potential absorption, and other supported order-flow events.
  • Configurable Audio Experiences: Adapt the audio presentation to the supported modes and level of detail appropriate for your workflow.
  • Learn While You Analyze: Reinforce tape-reading concepts while following the market instead of relying exclusively on visual inspection.

Why the Audio Feature Matters

Reading a tape requires more than recognizing numbers, colors, and signals. It requires understanding the relationship between price, participation, market structure, and the behavior of buyers and sellers.

B4 Tape is designed to help bridge that gap. Its combination of visual information and spoken context can make complex market activity easier to follow and provide an educational advantage for traders developing their tape-reading skills.
