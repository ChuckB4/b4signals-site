Reversal Bands. Middle Line. Trend Continuation.

B4 Swing Bands is a TradingView indicator that uses the highs and lows of the candles to measure trend reversals and trend continuation.

The band area warns that a trend reversal may be coming or that price may chop. The middle line is the trend-reversal price level, and the candles change color when the close is above or below it.

Core Capabilities

  • Swing Bands: a band area that shows a possible trend reversal ahead and possible chop.
  • Middle Line: the trend-reversal price level.
  • Trend Candles: candles change color when the close is above or below the middle line.
  • Long and Short Areas: long positions above the bands, short positions below them.
  • Trend Continuation: continuation is in effect when price pulls back into the bands and then breaks out in the direction of the trend.
  • Steadier Colors: updated to keep candle color changes to a minimum.

Reading the Bands

Price inside the bands means a reversal may be coming or the market may chop. Price above the bands favors longs and price below favors shorts. A pullback into the bands that breaks out again in the direction of the trend marks a trend continuation.
