The Integrated B4 Trading Analysis and Signal Engine

B4 Algo Pro is the core indicator and analytical engine of the B4 Signals ecosystem, available for NinjaTrader 8. It consolidates B4's proprietary indicator technology, market analysis, trend evaluation, signal generation, and integrated trading-context functionality into a unified analytical framework.

B4 Algo Pro is designed to do more than display indicator readings. It provides analytical information that can integrate with B4 Tape and compatible B4 tools to help traders interpret market conditions, evaluate trade opportunities, manage trade context, and identify potential profit-taking conditions.

Core Capabilities

  • Integrated Market Analysis: Combines B4 indicator functionality with trend, momentum, price action, and market-context analysis to help evaluate developing conditions.
  • Trend and Ichimoku Analysis: Incorporates trend structure, cloud context, and related analytical components into the consolidated B4 framework.
  • VWAP Integration: Provides volume-weighted price context to support the evaluation of price positioning and potential trading opportunities.
  • Machine-Learning-Assisted Signals: Integrates machine-learning signal functionality into the broader analytical process.
  • B4 Tape Integration: Supplies the analytical foundation for compatible B4 Tape functionality, helping connect indicator conditions, signals, and market context with the trader's order-flow and decision-support workflow.
  • B4 Brackets Integration: Supports the broader B4 trade-management workflow involving bracket functionality, subject to the relevant component's integration and configuration.
  • HUD Functionality: Supports compatible heads-up display functionality for presenting relevant trading information and conditions within the B4 environment.
  • Profit-Taking Triggers: Supports compatible trigger functionality for identifying or communicating potential profit-taking conditions as price develops and trade objectives are approached.
  • B4 Ecosystem Compatibility: Serves as a central analytical component within the B4 Signals suite, working alongside compatible tools such as B4 Tape, B4 L2 DOM Pro, and B4 Scanner.

New on NinjaTrader 8: 24/7 Brackets

  • 24/7 Brackets: B4 Brackets now run around the clock, through the overnight session as well as market hours, and B4 Tape reads them too.
  • Live Bracket Readout: The HUD shows the active bracket's Buy Above and Sell Below levels at all times, or a plain status when the market is closed.
  • Session Countdown: The Remaining row counts down to the next bracket rotation, the end of the brackets, the market open or the overnight reopen.
  • Holiday and Half-Day Calendar: Rotations and the countdown follow your chart's trading hours, so holidays and early closes are handled for you.
  • Rotation Alerts: Optional alerts and sound at each bracket rotation, with an early warning if you want one.

One Integrated Analytical Framework

B4 Algo Pro is the foundation; compatible B4 tools extend how its analytical information is presented and used. B4 Tape provides a dedicated market-analysis and order-flow interface, B4 L2 DOM Pro provides an execution-focused interface, and B4 Scanner provides a market-scanning interface. B4 Brackets, HUD functionality, and profit-taking triggers complement the broader analytical and trade-management workflow where supported.
