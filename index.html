The Tape is the heart of B4 Order Flow, built around a powerful idea: traders should learn to understand what the market is doing, why it matters, and how the evidence connects to their trading system.

Its integrated Audio Squawk Box and tape-reading coach brings market commentary and educational analysis together, connecting Auction Market Theory, footprint reading, volume and TPO profiling, market microstructure, Ichimoku, and the broader B4 methodology.

With a library of 224 spoken order-flow statements across 11 categories, the Tape is designed to help traders understand relationships between indicators, price levels, order flow, and developing market conditions—not simply react to isolated alerts.

The Audio Squawk Box — Learn to Read the Market

The squawk box is the centerpiece of the Tape. Its purpose goes beyond announcing that a signal fired: it helps explain the relevant evidence and reinforce the principles used to interpret it.

Market Microstructure

Confluence-Based Learning

Understand how multiple pieces of evidence can reinforce, weaken, or contradict a developing market scenario.

224 Spoken Statements. 11 Order-Flow Categories.

Recognize the interaction. Understand the context. Evaluate the confirmation.

The Tape's squawk library holds 224 statements in 11 categories, from order flow, footprint and absorption to the auction, swings, regime and confluence, built around the indicators and market information available on your chart.

Rather than evaluating each signal in isolation, these statements focus on relationships among price action, order flow, market structure, and the B4 analytical ecosystem.

The objective is to help traders understand what evidence is present, what confirmation may be missing, and when conflicting signals warrant caution.

Examples of the concepts covered include:
  • Absorption and potential exhaustion of aggressive buying or selling.
  • Footprint imbalances and changes in buying or selling pressure.
  • Failed auctions and breakout acceptance or rejection.
  • Liquidity walls, vacuums, and shifts in tape speed.
  • Volume and TPO profile relationships around important market levels.
  • Squeeze conditions and potential breakout scenarios.
  • Chikou Span relationships, B4 Trend Line conditions, absorption, and relevant support or resistance zones.
  • Confluence involving B4 Algo Pro, B4 Indicator Pro, B4 Ichimoku, B4 Auction Pro, VWAP and other configured analytical tools.

Learn Your B4 System — Not Just the Indicators

The Tape is designed to connect technical information with the methodology behind its interpretation. Its educational approach distinguishes established B4 teaching principles from software-generated observations, helping users understand the difference between what an indicator reports, what the combined evidence suggests, and what still requires confirmation.

Signal Context, Confirmation, and Follow-Through

The Tape is designed to distinguish between a signal firing, a potential setup developing, confirmation being established, and an actual trade being executed. The Tape keeps these events clearly separate.

When supported data is available, commentary can explain the relevant signal, direction, price, source, and evidence, including subsequent confirmation or failure where the configured workflow supports it.

When information is missing, invalid, or conflicting, the Tape is designed to say so rather than present it as confirmed. The goal is clear, evidence-based market education—not unsupported predictions.

Informational by Design

The Tape is the informational and analytical side of B4 Order Flow. It complements the trader's existing chart and execution environment while keeping its role separate from order management.

New in the Tape — A Decision Engine That Explains Itself
