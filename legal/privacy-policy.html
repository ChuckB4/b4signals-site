Privacy Policy

TopTech Digital LLC · B4 Signals · Effective November 6, 2026.

TopTech Digital LLC · B4 Signals Effective date: November 6, 2026

This policy explains what personal data TopTech Digital LLC ("B4 Signals", "we", "us") collects, why we collect it, and the choices you have. It applies to b4signals.com, b4indicators.com, our member area members.b4signals.com (hosted on Kajabi) and our NinjaTrader software. No trading results leave your computer unless you explicitly opt in.

1. What we collect

Account and purchase data. When you buy or sign up, we collect your name, your email address, your billing country and postal code, what you bought, and your payment history. Card and PayPal details are handled by Kajabi's payment processors, for example Stripe or PayPal. We do not see or store your full card number.

License data. To license the software, we collect the NinjaTrader Machine ID of each computer you license (up to 2 per user), and we record it in NinjaTrader's vendor licensing system.

Messages. We keep the emails, Discord messages and support requests you send us.

Course activity. Kajabi records your logins and the lessons you open or complete.

Website data. Our sites and Kajabi record technical data such as your IP address, browser, device, the pages you view, and the site that referred you. They use cookies to keep you logged in and to measure site use. We may use advertising cookies, for example Google Ads remarketing, to show our ads to past visitors on other sites. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings, and you can opt out of personalized Google ads at adssettings.google.com.

2. Trading data and data sharing (opt-in only)

The B4 ecosystem learns from its members. B4 Quantum's models improve through shared trading data from participating members. Members may voluntarily opt in to share their Sim trading results. These contributions help train and refine the ecosystem's models, which benefits all B4 members. Data sharing is optional, and members can turn it off.

Our rules:

  1. Privacy: no trading results leave your computer without your explicit opt-in.
  2. Default: data sharing is OFF.
  3. Control: you can turn sharing on or off at any time.
  4. Disclosure: this policy names exactly what is collected, sent, kept and used for model training.
  5. NinjaTrader: no data-sharing function is switched on until NinjaTrader's disclosure requirements are met.

Model training (B4 Quantum and other B4 models). Not active yet. Today, no B4 product shares Sim results for model training. Before any product offers this, we will update this section with the exact list of fields, where the data goes and how long it is kept. The product will show you the same list before you opt in. Shared Sim results will be used only to train and improve B4 models.

B4 CoPilot Pro: optional AMP trade reports. B4 CoPilot Pro has a setting called "Enable AMP Reports (Beta)". It is OFF by default, and nothing is sent while it is off. Earlier versions: in B4 CoPilot Pro versions released before B4 V4, this setting was on by default. If you use an earlier version, open its settings and switch "Enable AMP Reports" off if you do not want reports sent. The B4 V4 update switches it off by default. If you turn it on, B4 CoPilot Pro sends a report over the internet each time a market order is submitted, and each time a limit or stop order fills or partly fills. Each report contains:

  • the strategy name;
  • the instrument (full contract name, for example "ES 12-26");
  • the order action and order name, and the order quantity;
  • the direction (buy or sell);
  • the last chart price at the moment of the report;
  • the order time stamp (UTC);
  • your NinjaTrader account display name;
  • your account profit and loss (realized plus unrealized, in U.S. dollars) and your account cash value;
  • your NinjaTrader Machine ID;
  • the AMP user name and AMP key you entered in the setting; and
  • a report counter.

The reports go to the AMP reporting server, which a third-party service provider hosts on Amazon Web Services in the United States. They are used to show your trades on the AMP dashboard. If you turn this on for a live account, that live account's data is sent. You can turn the setting off at any time in the strategy settings. Turning it off stops all future reports. To have reports already sent deleted, email info@b4signals.com.

Other products. Apart from AMP reports (if you turn them on) and NinjaTrader's license check, our software is not designed to send your trading, account or usage data off your computer. Status lights and "heartbeat" signals in our tools stay on your computer. To check your license, the software contacts NinjaTrader's licensing servers, and NinjaTrader's own privacy policy applies to that check.

3. How we use your data

We use your data to:

  • process purchases and subscriptions;
  • issue and check licenses;
  • give you access to the course;
  • provide support;
  • send receipts, renewal reminders, price-change notices and product updates (including NinjaTrader approval news);
  • prevent fraud and license sharing, and respond to payment disputes;
  • improve our sites and products; and
  • comply with the law.

We send marketing emails only where the law allows, and every marketing email has an unsubscribe link.

4. Who we share it with

We do not sell your personal data. We share it only:

  • with the service providers that run our business: Kajabi (member area, checkout, email), payment processors (Stripe, PayPal), NinjaTrader (licensing), Discord (community), Google (site analytics and ads), and, only if you turn on AMP reports, the AMP server provider;
  • when the law requires it, or to protect our rights, for example to respond to a chargeback with purchase, license and access records;
  • in a merger, an acquisition or a sale of our business, subject to this policy.

5. How long we keep it

We keep account, purchase and license records while your account is active, and afterwards for as long as tax, accounting and dispute rules require (usually 7 years for payment records). We keep support messages for up to 3 years. You can ask us to delete other data sooner (Section 7).

6. Security

We use reasonable safeguards and reputable providers. No internet transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security. Keep your login and AMP key private.

7. Your choices and rights

You can:

  • see the personal data we hold about you;
  • correct it;
  • delete it (we keep only the records the law requires); or
  • get a copy of it.

Email info@b4signals.com from your account email to make any of these requests. We do not charge a fee. We reply within 30 days. You can turn off data sharing and AMP reports in the software at any time, and unsubscribe from marketing emails using the link in each email. Depending on where you live (for example California or other U.S. states), you may have additional rights under local law. We honor those rights, and we will not treat you differently for using them.

8. Children

Our products are for adults. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18.

9. Links to other sites

Our sites link to other sites, such as NinjaTrader, YouTube and Discord. Their privacy policies apply to those sites. We are not responsible for their privacy practices.

10. Changes to this policy

We will post any changes on this page with a new effective date. If a change is material, for example a new kind of data sharing, we will email active customers before it takes effect. We will never start sharing your trading results without your opt-in.
