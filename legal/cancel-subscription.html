You can cancel a Monthly, Quarterly or Annual subscription at any time before your next renewal, without giving a reason. Cancelling stops all future charges. You keep access to the B4 tools and the course until the end of the period you have already paid for. After that, your subscription license ends. Cancelling does not refund the current period (see our Refund Policy).

To avoid the next charge, cancel before your next billing date. Your billing date is shown in your account and on your payment receipt.

If you pay by card (Kajabi checkout)

  1. Go to https://members.b4signals.com and log in.
  2. Click your profile icon in the top right corner.
  3. Select Settings, then open the Billing (or Payment / Subscriptions) section.
  4. Find the B4 subscription and select Cancel subscription.
  5. Confirm the cancellation. You will get a confirmation email. If you do not get one within 24 hours, email us.

If you pay with PayPal

You can cancel in your B4 account (the steps above) or in PayPal.

On a computer or mobile browser:

  1. Log in at paypal.com.
  2. Open Settings (the gear icon), then Payments.
  3. Select Manage automatic payments.
  4. Select TopTech Digital / B4 Signals, then Cancel, and confirm.

In the PayPal app:

  1. Open Settings (the gear icon).
  2. Select Automatic Payments.
  3. Select the B4 subscription.
  4. Select Cancel (or Remove PayPal as your payment method), then confirm.

Cancelling in PayPal stops future charges. If you also want your B4 account closed, email us.

Cancel by email

You can also cancel by emailing info@b4signals.com from the email address on your account, with "Cancel" in the subject line. Please include the name of your subscription. We confirm by email within 1 business day. The cancellation counts from the time we receive your email.

One-Time purchases

A One-Time purchase does not renew, so there is nothing to cancel.

Older plans

Older plans, for example "B4 Trading Tools Monthly" at $130, are cancelled the same way.
